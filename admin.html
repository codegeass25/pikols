Security & backup

Staff users & role access

Create Admin, Booking Manager, Event Manager and Scorer accounts.

Operational backup & restore

Creates a PIKOL operational backup containing courts and court slots, player accounts, bookings, payments and receipt files, memberships and plan definitions, tournaments/brackets/scoring history, Open Play, notifications and audit history. Not included: staff accounts (Admin, Booking Manager, Event Manager, Scorer), Settings/branding, and deployment/.env configuration.

Restore replaces the current operational data only. Settings/branding and staff access accounts remain unchanged; courts, schedules and other operating records are restored from the backup.

Reset operational data

Permanently removes client/player records, bookings, payments, transactions, tournament and Open Play records, registrations, receipt history, notification history, audit logs, legacy gateway history, and uploaded payment receipts. Only Settings/branding and staff access accounts are preserved. Courts, court slots and all other operating data are wiped.
