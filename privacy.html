PRIVACY & DATA USE

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PIKOL and WenJelly Sports Center collect, use, protect, and manage information used for account registration, bookings, events, payments, notifications, and authorized Google Gmail sending.

Effective: November 3, 2026Applies to the PIKOL booking website, PWA, and related management functions.

1. Who we are

PIKOL is the booking and sports-management system used by WenJelly Sports Center. It supports player accounts, court reservations, tournaments, Open Play, memberships, payment verification, operational notifications, and related venue services.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the feature you use, we may collect or process:

  • account details such as full name, email address, mobile number, password hash, account status, and profile picture;
  • a Valid ID submitted for new-account identity verification, where required;
  • booking, tournament, Open Play, membership, attendance, scoring, and participation records;
  • payment-related information such as payment method, amount, uploaded receipt or screenshot, verification status, and fraud-prevention records;
  • communications and notification preferences;
  • technical and security data such as session information, timestamps, audit records, and limited device/browser information needed to operate and protect the service.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate the PIKOL service, verify accounts and payments, manage bookings and sports activities, provide membership and event functions, send service-related notices, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security and audit records, respond to support concerns, and improve the reliability of the system.

4. Profile pictures and Valid IDs

Profile pictures are used as player account images within PIKOL. Valid IDs are used only for identity and account-verification purposes, fraud prevention, dispute handling, and related security or administrative needs. Valid IDs are treated as private records and are not intended for public player profiles.

5. Google OAuth and Gmail data

PIKOL uses Google authorization only for an authorized WenJelly management account that connects Gmail as the system email sender. Ordinary player accounts are not required to connect their Google account.

When Management connects Gmail, PIKOL requests only the Google permissions needed for this integration:

  • openid and email — to identify the Google account connected as the sender;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send — to send PIKOL service emails through that authorized Gmail account.

PIKOL uses the connected Gmail account to send operational emails such as account, booking, payment, tournament, Open Play, membership, and administrative notifications. PIKOL does not request permission to read, modify, or delete the Gmail inbox through this integration.

The OAuth refresh token needed to maintain the Gmail connection is stored on the PIKOL backend and is not exposed to ordinary users. Google user data obtained through OAuth is not sold and is not used for advertising.

6. Sharing of information

Information may be accessible to authorized WenJelly management or staff members who need it to operate the facility and PIKOL. We may also use service providers that are necessary to operate communications, hosting, security, or payment-related functions. We do not sell personal information or Google user data.

7. Data retention

We retain records for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were collected, including account administration, booking and payment history, identity verification, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, audit requirements, security, and other legitimate operational or legal needs. Records may be deleted or anonymized when they are no longer reasonably required, subject to applicable obligations and system integrity requirements.

8. Security

PIKOL uses administrative access controls, authenticated routes, password hashing, private storage for sensitive uploads, logging, and other technical safeguards appropriate to the system. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should also protect their account credentials and devices.

9. Your choices and requests

You may contact WenJelly Sports Center to ask about personal information associated with your account, request corrections, raise a privacy concern, or request deletion where appropriate. Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, transaction history, legal obligations, or legitimate operational purposes.

10. Third-party services

PIKOL may interact with third-party services such as Google APIs, email/SMS delivery providers, hosting infrastructure, or payment services. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies when their services are used.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PIKOL features, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are published.

12. Contact

For privacy or data-related questions, contact WenJelly Sports Center at wenjellysportscenter@gmail.com, or use the contact information displayed on the PIKOL booking site.

Google authorization note: The Google account connection in PIKOL is an Admin/Management feature used to send system emails. It is separate from normal player sign-in and registration.
